Screening Form

Before you begin

These questionnaires are provided in English by Tally. They are screening tools, not a diagnosis or emergency service. Your answers go to the external form provider. Before answering, read the MindCheck privacy notice.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, do not wait for screening results. Seek urgent in-person help or contact local emergency services. In Nigeria, call 112.
