Screening Form

Before you begin

These screening questionnaires are currently provided in English by an external form service (Tally). They are screening tools, not a diagnosis or emergency service. Your responses are submitted to the external form provider; please review the MindCheck privacy notice before continuing.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, do not wait for screening results. Contact local emergency services or seek urgent in-person help. In Nigeria, call 112.
