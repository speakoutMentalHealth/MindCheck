PCL-5 — PTSD Checklist for DSM-5

The PCL-5 is a 20-item self-report instrument covering PTSD symptoms. It is a screening and symptom-monitoring aid, not a stand-alone PTSD diagnosis. Interpretation and follow-up should be clinician-guided.

Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACE)

ACEs describe certain potentially traumatic experiences during childhood. The number of experiences does not determine a person's future, diagnose a condition or measure their worth. Participation should always be optional, with clear privacy safeguards.

Learn about ACEs from the CDC ↗

Support and next steps

  • If symptoms such as intrusive memories, avoidance, sleep problems or feeling constantly on edge disrupt daily life, consult a qualified clinician.
  • You do not have to describe a traumatic event to use this page.
  • If you are in immediate danger, seek emergency assistance or go to the nearest emergency department.
