ADULT ALCOHOL-USE AWARENESS

WHO AUDIT Screening

A ten-question alcohol-use screening tool developed by the World Health Organization. Complete the original form first, then enter the total here for private, non-diagnostic guidance.

Privacy: MindCheck does not collect or store your AUDIT answers or total. This page only processes a number in the current browser session. It is not monitored by medical staff.

Step 1 · Complete the official AUDIT

Open the WHO manual and use its Appendix B self-report questionnaire, including the printed response options and scoring instructions. Please use all ten questions rather than the three-item AUDIT-C.

Open official WHO AUDIT ↗

The original questionnaire remains on WHO's website; it is not reproduced or modified here. For questions about alcohol use, a healthcare professional can help you complete and interpret it.

Step 2 · Privately understand your total

After scoring all ten answers using the WHO instructions, enter the full AUDIT total (0–40). This is not a new diagnostic questionnaire.

Do not enter a three-question AUDIT-C result here.

The WHO manual describes screening ranges as a guide for follow-up, not a diagnosis. A higher score calls for professional discussion; personal circumstances can change the interpretation.

Step 3 · Get support

If drinking affects your health, family life, work or safety, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional even if your score is low. Sudden cessation after heavy, dependent drinking can lead to dangerous withdrawal; seek medical advice before stopping abruptly.

Prepare for a professional assessment.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department.
