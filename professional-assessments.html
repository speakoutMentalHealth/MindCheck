DSM-5 Cross-Cutting Symptom Measures

These measures cover several symptom domains and can help guide a clinician's follow-up questions. They do not independently establish a diagnosis. Some domains, including suicidal thoughts and substance use, require a defined response pathway.

MMPI-2 / MMPI-3

These are professional psychological assessment inventories. MindCheck does not reproduce their restricted items, interpret their clinical scales, or offer them as unsupervised self-tests.

MMSE & MoCA cognitive measures

These tools can help qualified professionals evaluate concerns about memory and thinking. Administration, reproduction and scoring are subject to their respective licensing and training requirements. Neither is a general online IQ test.

When to seek help

If attention, memory, mood, behavior or other symptoms affect daily functioning, speak with a qualified health professional. New or sudden confusion or neurological symptoms require urgent medical assessment.
