## Workflow before any digital referral activation

1. Obtain explicit written agreement from each institution and specify accepted ages, presenting needs and clinical scope.
2. Obtain a named primary receiving officer plus deputy and approved contact channels.
3. Confirm ordinary clinic hours and urgent/emergency reception separately.
4. Agree how cases involving suicidal thoughts or immediate danger bypass routine booking and receive human clinical attention.
5. Test a synthetic referral with each site and confirm acknowledgment, fallback and escalation time targets.
6. Obtain a consent/privacy and data-retention plan approved by both institutions. No screening responses, phone numbers or patient names go to website analytics.
7. Publish only confirmed, authorized public contact information. Do not imply that a listing is staffed 24/7 without facility confirmation.
8. Check youth safeguarding, caregiver consent and services appropriate to age.
9. Have a qualified clinician review the screening tools and referral thresholds before enabling any self-administered high-risk assessment.
