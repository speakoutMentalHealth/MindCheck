## Why this campaign exists

Lead with **"Your mind matters. Check in with yourself."** and the approved MindCheck positioning **"Towards a sound mind and healthy growth."**

The value proposition is a simple, accessible space to pause, reflect, use self-care tools, and explore educational resources. Avoid clinical benefit claims, panic-based messages, and presentation as a substitute for professional care.

### Claims allowed in copy, based on public UI reviewed 2026-11-04

- Mood check-ins and **My Buddy** diary/checklist
- Breathing and grounding exercises
- Wellness tips and optional daily challenges
- Educational assessment/screening resources, with clear non-diagnostic notice
- Basic support information and self-directed navigation
- Phone Home Screen installation **only once device-specific install QA passes**

### Claims to avoid until separately verified

- Diagnosis, therapy, treatment, guaranteed counselling, 24/7 hotline or emergency service
- Confirmed partner hospital referrals, clinical monitoring, private community for all users
- Certificates, verified credentials, cash prizes, premiums or AI features as an unconditional entitlement
- Full translations into Hausa, Igbo, Yoruba, Pidgin and French without reviewed screen-by-screen QA
- "100% anonymous", "all data stays on your phone": external Tally questionnaires and optional contact/report tools use external providers
- Any impact, recovery, reach or adoption number not backed by documentation

## 30-second first Reel — exact production storyboard

**0–4s:** Adult Nigerian commuter/student or everyday street-life cutaways. Voice: "We check our messages, our plans and our work."  
**4–7s:** Cut to quiet shot and clear title "But when last did you check in with yourself?"  
**7–13s:** Real screen recording of **My Buddy**: mood check and diary (use a fictional demo account with no real health entries). Voice: "MindCheck gives you a space to notice how you feel."  
**13–20s:** Real screens of wellness tips and breathing. Voice: "Find a simple wellbeing tip. Pause for a breathing exercise."  
**20–26s:** Educational assessment centre screen. Voice: "Explore educational screening resources—and know their limits. They are not diagnoses."  
**26–30s:** Logo provided by the organisation, URL, slogan. Voice: "MindCheck by SpeakOut. Your mind matters. Check in with yourself."

**On-screen footer:** "Self-awareness and educational tools. Not medical diagnosis or emergency care."  
**Accessibility:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, captions embedded, large mobile legible text, 30fps, no flashing visual, avoid unlicensed music, show real verified UI rather than AI-simulated screenshots.

## Distribution and measurement

- Do **not** request screenshots of people's moods, questionnaire results, diaries or diagnoses.
- Only publish to opt-in WhatsApp groups and channels where sharing is welcomed.
- Use platform-reported **outbound link clicks**, content reach, shares and engagement as acquisition measurements.
- GoatCounter's MindCheck privacy wrapper removes page query strings, so do **not** claim UTM parameters are captured or that pageviews identify unique people.
- On a daily log record: date, platform, link clicks (where supported), source, content ID, public pageviews (aggregate), safety complaints, follow-up status and screenshot/receipt reference.
- Weekly review: identify content with useful reach, assess actual visits in aggregate, fix friction, and decide which formats to repeat. Do not infer mental-health outcomes from click data.
- Sample non-sensitive tracking link: https://mindcheck.speakoutmentalhealth.org/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mindcheck_nov2026&utm_content=wave1_launch . Since MindCheck strips query strings from site analytics, this is a portable marketing label rather than verified attribution.
