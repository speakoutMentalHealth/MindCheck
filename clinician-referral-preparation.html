Clinician referral preparation

A practical checklist for trained healthcare personnel reviewing whether a referral pathway is ready. This is not a referral submission system or a substitute for clinical judgment.

Not connected to any hospital: FMC Keffi and Nagari Allah Magani Hospital are proposed partners only. Do not enter patient names, test answers, contact information or clinical notes on this page. No data is submitted or saved.

Relevant clinical resources

WHO's mhGAP Intervention Guide supports assessment and management by nonspecialist healthcare providers. Use the applicable current national protocols, local scope of practice, supervision and safety procedures.

This checklist does not perform diagnosis, risk scoring or clinical triage.

Referral activation requirements

Before MindCheck offers a direct referral channel, SpeakOut must verify provider approval, intake hours, safe data handling, emergency escalation and a responsible clinical lead. Until then, no online referral is available.
