Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how SpeakOut MindCheck (“we”, “our”, “us”) handles information when you use the MindCheck web app, including screenings, volunteer training, and monitoring tools.

Aligned with Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) & Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA)

1. Who we are

SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach is a Nigerian non-profit initiative providing school and community mental-health education, digital screenings, and safe volunteer support pathways.

Adult access and the MindCheck Guide

MindCheck is for adults aged 18 and over. An age confirmation is kept for this browser tab’s session where storage is available; we do not request a date of birth or identity document. This is self-declaration, not verified identity.

The Guide currently displays prepared platform answers on your device. Optional Gemini explanations remain disabled until a server connection is configured. If enabled, only a selected platform question identifier and language are sent to our guide service; personal mood history, screening answers, reports and contact details are excluded. Google’s unpaid services may use submitted content and responses to improve products and may use human review. You can use the prepared answers without AI.

2. What information we collect

MindCheck is designed to minimise personal data. We generally collect:

  • On-device wellbeing data: mood check-ins, completed tips and badges stay in this browser. Mood check-ins are not automatically sent to SpeakOut or Google Apps Script. Other people using this browser may see your saved history.
  • Non-identifying logs: short notes such as “anxiety & sleep — shared breathing exercise; offered clinic list” without names or contact details.
  • Certificate data (if you request a certificate): your name, quiz score, certificate ID, and date of issue.
  • Optional aggregate traffic statistics: when configured, GoatCounter counts visits to public information and learning pages, with approximate device, browser and location statistics. We do not send names, account identifiers, mood entries, screening answers, report text, certificate IDs or form contents to analytics. Private support, screening, reporting, monitoring and certificate pages are excluded. Page queries and fragments are removed; external referrers are reduced to their website origin. Our integration uses no analytics cookies or persistent visitor identifiers, and respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Analytics blockers and offline visits can reduce the totals. Visitor estimates are not login counts.

Mood check-ins do not ask for your name or contact details. A safety report may include optional contact details if you choose to request follow-up.

External screening form provider

When you choose to open MindCheck's screening questionnaires, the form is provided by Tally, an external service. Responses you enter into the questionnaire are submitted to that provider and handled according to its own privacy practices and the form owner's configuration. This is separate from the mood check-ins and saved tips kept in your browser. Please review the form's privacy information before entering sensitive details. SpeakOut should review the form's ownership, access controls and retention settings before relying on it for clinical or programme decisions.

Reports and direct contact

If you choose to submit a safety report, the report contents and any optional contact details are sent to the report service. Reports do not include your mood check-in device identifier. Phone, email and WhatsApp enquiries use their respective communication services. These are separate from on-device mood history and are not emergency response services.

3. How we use the information

  • To provide mood check-ins, resources, and volunteer training content.
  • To monitor core indicators (number of contacts, screenings, referrals, follow-ups) for programme quality and grant reporting in aggregated form.
  • To issue and verify volunteer training certificates using certificate IDs.
  • To improve the app safely (for example, usability and performance analytics).

4. Legal basis and consent

For Nigerian users, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interest: providing low-barrier mental-health support tools with minimal data.
  • Consent: when you enter your name for a certificate or choose to contact us directly by email, phone, or WhatsApp.

5. Data retention

  • Browser-stored data (mood history, points, saved tips) stays on your device until you clear your browser storage.
  • Programme monitoring logs and certificate registers are retained only as long as needed for reporting and audit, then aggregated or anonymised.
  • We aim to review raw logs regularly and delete any information that is no longer required.

6. Sharing and security

  • We do not sell your data.
  • Limited volunteers and coordinators may see programme-level aggregates (for example, total screenings per school).
  • Where we use cloud tools (for example, Google Workspace, Plausible), we choose providers with strong security and data-protection practices.

7. Your rights

Under NDPR / NDPA and similar frameworks, you can:

  • Ask what information we hold about you (for example, certificate records).
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal details where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (for example, close a certificate record linked to your name, if lawful to do so).

To exercise these rights, contact us at speaaakout@gmail.com.

8. Adults and safeguarding

MindCheck’s tools are for adults aged 18 and over. SpeakOut’s separate school and young learners’ programmes are accessed through the main SpeakOut website. Where an adult reports a safeguarding concern involving another person, appropriate escalation may still be needed. Do not include identifying details about a child in the Guide.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Notice as our programme grows or as regulations change. Major changes will be announced through our usual communication channels.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this notice or data protection at SpeakOut MindCheck, please email speaaakout@gmail.com.
