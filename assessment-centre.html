WHO assessment and clinical guidance

Official resources complement MindCheck screening. Some are designed for trained healthcare workers, not as self-diagnosis questionnaires.

  • WHO-5 Well-Being Index: Five-item wellbeing tool; available through the existing screening pathway. Original WHO-5 reference ↗.
  • WHO mhGAP Intervention Guide: Clinical assessment and management guidance for nonspecialist healthcare providers, including depression, substance use, and self-harm/suicide. Official WHO mhGAP guide ↗. This is a clinical resource, not a public scored test.

Planned clinical assessment pathways

These are not yet available as questionnaires. We will first confirm clinical suitability, approved wording, licensing and safety requirements.

  • Suicide-risk assessment: evaluate C-SSRS with PHC/FMC clinical partners and implement only after escalation contacts, operating hours and emergency response are confirmed.
  • Children and teenagers: develop a separate, age-appropriate approach with clinical supervision, caregiver/teacher guidance and consent protections.

When to seek professional support

If symptoms interfere with school, work, sleep, relationships or everyday activities, consider speaking with a qualified health professional even when a screening tool suggests a lower level of concern. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency service.
