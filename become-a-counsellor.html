A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you begin.

Will this make me a professional counsellor?+

No. The foundation course builds mental health literacy and support skills. Professional counselling requires appropriate formal training, supervised practice and any registration applicable to the role.

How do I receive my certificate?+

Complete the Be There course on the provider’s website and follow its certificate instructions. MindCheck does not issue the provider’s certificate or receive your results automatically.

Has my certificate been submitted here?+

No. This page has no certificate upload. The checklist is only a personal reminder. Earlier “saved on this device” entries were not sent to SpeakOut. Use Contact SpeakOut and send your enquiry from your email app; attach a certificate there if you choose.

What if I want support for myself?+

You don’t need to complete training to ask for help. Explore MindCheck support options. Training enquiries are not an emergency response service.
