Volunteer Training Programme

✅ Reward: “Volunteer Trained” +50 points

This joint volunteer training prepares you to support people safely across Nigerian campuses, communities, outreach and humanitarian projects. Complete the lessons, pass the quiz, and unlock your in-app Volunteer Trained badge plus a digital certificate issued by SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach, Synia Aid Foundation & CNU Medical Institue.

About this partnership

MindCheck training is a joint initiative of three Nigerian NGOs working together to support young people, families and communities:

  • SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach – campus and community mental health education, safe listening + referral skills.
  • Synia Aid Foundation – education and poverty-relief support for vulnerable families and learners.
  • CNU Medical Institue – humanitarian healthcare development focused on medical training and community health initiatives.
