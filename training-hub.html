LESSON 1 OF 5

Lesson 1 — Safe Listening Basics (L-I-S-T-E-N)

Safe listening helps someone feel calm enough to choose safer next steps. You’re not “fixing” them — you’re creating safety, clarity, and connection.

The L-I-S-T-E-N method

  • L — Listen: Full attention. Allow silence.
  • I — Inquire: Open questions: “What has been hardest?”
  • S — Summarize: “So school pressure + money worries are affecting sleep.”
  • T — Thank: “Thank you for trusting me.”
  • E — Empathize: “That sounds heavy.”
  • N — Next step: One small step for today.
Example: “I’m tired of everything.” → “I’m glad you told me. What has today felt like?” → summarize → empathize → choose a small next step.

LESSON 2 OF 5

Lesson 2 — Red Flags & Immediate Danger

Most chats are not emergencies — but some require immediate escalation.

Immediate red flags

  • Self-harm plan or intent
  • Violence/abuse happening now
  • Child/vulnerable person at risk
  • Severe confusion/hallucinations commanding harm

What to do

  • Stay present: “Your safety matters.”
  • Call 112
  • Escalate to safeguarding lead
  • Log minimum facts + actions taken
Example: “I bought pills.” → “Are you alone? I’m calling 112 now. Stay with me.”

LESSON 3 OF 5

Lesson 3 — Boundaries & Ethical Referral

Boundaries protect you and the person. Ethical referral means choices, consent, and dignity.

Scope

  • Supportive listening + calming strategies
  • Offer options: clinic, counsellor, trusted adult

Do not

  • Diagnose/prescribe
  • Promise outcomes
  • Become a 24/7 hotline
Referral script: “Would you like 2–3 support options? You can choose what feels safest.”

LESSON 4 OF 5

Lesson 4 — Privacy-First Logging

Record only what is needed for safety + learning.

Log

  • Date/time
  • General concern
  • Action taken

Do not log

  • Full names/addresses
  • Screenshots
  • Judgments
Good log: “2026-01-02 • exam stress + poor sleep • taught breathing • offered clinic list • person chose WhatsApp support.”

LESSON 5 OF 5

Lesson 5 — Cultural Sensitivity & Volunteer Self-Care

Respect improves trust and outcomes. Your wellbeing matters too.

Cultural sensitivity

  • Ask preferences: language/name
  • Respect faith + offer additional options
  • Avoid labels; use neutral language

Self-care

  • Debrief with supervisor
  • Set limits
  • Escalate when overwhelmed
Example: “Counselling is shameful.” → “Many people combine faith + family support + counselling. Would you like private options?”
