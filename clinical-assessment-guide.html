Prepare for a professional assessment

A screening form cannot diagnose a condition. Use these steps to prepare for an appointment with a qualified healthcare professional.

Privacy: This page does not collect, save, transmit or send health information to SpeakOut. You do not need to provide trauma details online.

Step 1 · Choose the right service

For trauma or PTSD symptoms, ask for a clinician trained in mental health or trauma assessment. For broad psychiatric symptoms, speak with a mental health professional about a comprehensive interview. Primary healthcare providers can advise on an appropriate referral.

SpeakOut is verifying partner PHC and medical-centre contacts, opening hours and emergency arrangements before offering direct referrals. Until then, contact a qualified local healthcare provider independently; MindCheck does not transmit referrals or provide live clinical monitoring.

Step 2 · Bring information you choose to share

  • Note which symptoms affect your daily functioning and approximately when they began.
  • If you've already completed an official PCL-5 or other instrument, you may bring your own copy to your clinician.
  • Mention medication, sleep concerns or other health conditions that may affect symptoms.
  • You can decline to discuss specific traumatic details in a public or online setting.

Step 3 · Ask about assessment and follow-up

  • Which clinical interview or assessment is appropriate?
  • Who can discuss screening results and other possible explanations?
  • What treatment and follow-up options are available?
  • How should I seek urgent help if my safety changes before the next appointment?

For healthcare professionals

Use the clinical referral preparation checklist to review consent, clinical responsibilities and facility readiness before any eventual handoff. It is guidance only and does not send patient details.

What to expect from future referrals

Verified provider details will be shown only after the facility confirms that it accepts referrals. Any future request-to-refer feature will clearly explain what information is shared, ask your permission and provide a fallback if the facility is unavailable. No referral form is active on this page today.

Urgent safety

If you or another person may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department now. Do not wait for an online result or referral form.
